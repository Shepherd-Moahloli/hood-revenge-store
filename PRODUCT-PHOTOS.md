# Model photos on product pages

Place photos into public/images/products/<product-id>/.

For Signal Hoodie, use:
- public/images/products/signal-hoodie/01-front.jpg
- public/images/products/signal-hoodie/02-back.jpg
- public/images/products/signal-hoodie/03-side.jpg
- public/images/products/signal-hoodie/04-detail.jpg

Each product already has a folder. Supported formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP and AVIF. Number filenames to control thumbnail order. Descriptive filenames become accessible image descriptions. The first photo becomes the main image. Photos are shown fully, without cropping, in a portrait frame. Click thumbnails or swipe to change angles.

Refresh the product page during local development after adding files. Rebuild and redeploy for the hosted website. This is a local file workflow, not a public upload form.

Until photos are supplied for a product, the front/back concept illustrations remain as placeholders. Catalogue cards remain illustrations.
